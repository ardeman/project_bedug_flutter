Bedug calls you to prayer like the mosque drum it's named after. Accurate times wherever you are, a countdown to the next prayer, the Quran, dhikr, a Hijri calendar and a Qibla compass, on your phone and your computer.

FREE, NO ADS, PRIVATE
No account and no ads. Everything is calculated on your device and works offline. Your reading, bookmarks and fasting log never leave it.

PRAYER TIMES
• Kemenag RI by default, or one of nine global methods such as Muslim World League, ISNA and Umm al-Qura, with the Shafi'i or Hanafi Asr
• Imsak, sunrise, an estimated Dhuha start and the last third of the night
• Location from GPS, or pick your city and keep location off
• A live countdown to the next prayer

REMINDERS
• A notification for each prayer, with an early reminder 5 to 30 minutes before
• A bedug drum, a siren, the system sound, or (on Android) any tone on your phone
• Sahur, sunnah fasts (Monday and Thursday, Ayyamul Bidh, Arafah, Ashura) and Islamic holidays, each optional
• Home screen widgets: the next prayer, the ayah of the day and a Ramadan countdown

QURAN
• The full Uthmani text with Saheeh International's English translation
• Page mode following the 604 pages of the Madinah mushaf
• Bookmarks, continue reading and a khatam planner from 7 to 60 days
• An ayah of the day chosen for the occasion, with the reason why

DHIKR AND DUA
• Morning and evening dhikr from Hisnul Muslim, Al-Ma'thurat or a ratib, with a counter on each recitation
• Dhikr after prayer, daily duas and the 99 Names of Allah

ISLAMIC CALENDAR
• Hijri dates from MABIMS by default, or Umm al-Qura, with a ±2 day adjustment
• Islamic holidays and how many days away they are

RAMADAN AND ZAKAT
• A countdown to imsak and iftar
• A Ramadan fasting log that tracks the make-up (qada) fasts you owe
• A zakat al-mal and zakat al-fitr calculator

QIBLA
A Qibla compass on your phone. On a computer, Bedug shows the bearing, the distance to Makkah and the Qibla's angle from the sun.

In English, Indonesian, Arabic and Malay, with a full right-to-left layout in Arabic.

Bedug is free and stays free. If you like, you can give an infaq to support Bedug's development.
