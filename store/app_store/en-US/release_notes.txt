• Bedug checks for updates when it opens (you can turn this off in Settings), and Settings has a Check for updates button. Copies from GitHub download, verify and install the update once you confirm; store copies update through Google Play or the App Store.
• A control-by-control app tour highlights each feature, scrolls it into view and explains it in a small nearby bubble. Permission requests still wait for your acknowledgement.
• Hijri dates follow the MABIMS calendar by default, as Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei and Singapore reckon them. Umm al-Qura stays available in Settings.
• Subuh uses a bedtime icon and Maghrib a sunset icon consistently in the app, website, home screen widgets and lock screen widgets.
• On a half-folded iPhone Duo, the Quran's mushaf pages fill the screen.
• If location access was permanently denied, turning automatic location back on now offers to open Bedug's app settings.
• If notifications are disabled in system settings, choosing Continue now opens Bedug's notification settings.
• Notification switches now reflect system permission, including after choosing Not now or returning from system settings.
• The next-prayer countdown no longer restarts over and over after you travel to another time zone.
